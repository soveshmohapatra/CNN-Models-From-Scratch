How do neural networks see images?

They learned to scan with sliding windows.

Before CNNs, computer vision depended on hand-crafted features—SIFT, HOG, edge detectors designed by humans. Brittle, domain-specific, and unable to generalize.

Convolutional Neural Networks changed everything by learning features directly from data, discovering hierarchical patterns from edges to textures to complete objects.

From ImageNet to medical imaging, CNNs power virtually every modern vision system. But how do they actually work? What does a convolution really compute?
